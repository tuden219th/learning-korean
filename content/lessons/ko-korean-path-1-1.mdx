# 주어 표시 하는 입자: 이/가

## 목표 (Mục Tiêu / Learning Objectives)

After this lesson, you will:
- Understand that Korean uses **particles** (입자) to show grammatical relationships
- Recognize the **subject marker 이/가** in Korean sentences
- Use 이/가 correctly in simple Korean sentences

## Khác Biệt với Tiếng Việt (How Korean Differs from Vietnamese)

**Vietnamese:** Word order shows relationships
- Tôi đi học. (I go study.) — Subject comes first by position

**Korean:** Particles show relationships
- 나**가** 학교에 가요. (I go to school.) — The particle 가 marks "I" as the subject, even if word order changes

This is a **critical difference** for Vietnamese learners. In Korean, the particle does the job, not word order.

## Khái Niệm (The Concept)

In Korean, **particles** are small words attached to nouns to show their grammatical function:
- 이/가 = marks the **subject** (the one doing the action)

### Examples

**Example 1: Subject marker with 가다 (go)**
```
나가 학교에 가요.
(I go to school.)

나  = I
가  = subject marker
학교 = school
에   = location particle
가요 = go (casual polite)
```

**Example 2: Subject marker with 있다 (exist/have)**
```
고양이가 예뻐요.
(The cat is pretty.)

고양이 = cat
가    = subject marker
예뻐요 = (is) pretty
```

## Quy Tắc (Rules)

**Rule 1: Choose the particle based on the final sound of the noun**
- If the noun ends in a **consonant**: use **이**
  - Example: 책이 있어요 (There's a book.)
- If the noun ends in a **vowel**: use **가**
  - Example: 나가 간다 (I go.) or 고양이가 있어요 (A cat exists.)

**Rule 2: The particle is attached directly to the noun**
```
Subject Noun + 이/가 + Rest of Sentence

나 (I/vowel) → 나가
책 (book/consonant) → 책이
학생 (student/consonant) → 학생이
```

## Thực Hành (Practice)

**Practice 1: Identify the subject**

1. **누가** 학교에 가요? → Who goes to school?
   - Answer: 나**가** 학교에 가요 (I go.)

2. **누가** 밥을 먹어요? → Who eats rice?
   - Answer: 내 친구**가** 밥을 먹어요 (My friend eats.)

**Practice 2: Choose the correct particle**

1. 나____ (은/가?) 학생입니다. → I am a student.
   - **Correct: 나가 학생입니다.** (나 ends in vowel → 가)

2. 책____ (이/가?) 있어요. → There's a book.
   - **Correct: 책이 있어요.** (책 ends in consonant → 이)

## 흔한 실수 (Common Vietnamese Learner Errors)

❌ **Mistake 1: Confusing with 은/는 (topic marker)**
- **WRONG:** 나는 학교에 가요... wait, what's the particle doing?
- **CORRECT:** 나**가** 학교에 가요. (I [subject] go to school.)

❌ **Mistake 2: Forgetting the particle entirely**
- **WRONG:** 나 학교에 가요. (Sounds incomplete to Korean speakers)
- **CORRECT:** 나**가** 학교에 가요.

## 요약 (Summary)

The subject particle **이/가** marks who or what does the action in a Korean sentence. This is one of the most fundamental Korean grammar concepts and is essential for understanding Korean grammar structure.
