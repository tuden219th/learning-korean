# Grade 10 Korean Entrance Examination Competency Standards

**Status:** PENDING OFFICIAL EXAM ANALYSIS

## Purpose

This document defines two separate competency standards derived from the official Grade 10 Korean entrance examination:

1. **EXAM READINESS** — What a student must know to perform well on the entrance exam
2. **LANGUAGE DEVELOPMENT** — Additional Korean ability that is educationally valuable but not required for the exam

These standards are mutually exclusive and serve different curriculum goals.

## LANGUAGE DEVELOPMENT STANDARD

**Definition:** Additional Korean ability that develops communicative competence, cultural understanding, or language acquisition beyond what the entrance exam directly requires, but has clear educational value.

### Why Include This?

- Students who pass the exam may want to continue learning Korean
- Language development supports deeper engagement and retention
- Communicative ability enhances practical utility

## Classification System

Each curriculum item will be labeled:

- **[E]** = EXAM READINESS (required for entrance exam)
- **[D]** = LANGUAGE DEVELOPMENT (educational value, not required for exam)
- **[E+D]** = Both (required for exam AND has language development value)

---

## Curriculum Implication

**Korean Path Curriculum Will Contain:**

1. **Core Track [E]:** All exam-required competencies
2. **Extension Track [D]:** Optional language development modules
3. **Integrated [E+D]:** Lessons that serve both purposes
