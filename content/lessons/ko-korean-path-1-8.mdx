# 부정: 못 vs 안

## 목표 (Mục Tiêu / Learning Objectives)

After this lesson, you will:

* Understand **못** as a negation marker expressing inability or lack of opportunity
* Review **안** as a negation marker for choosing not to do something
* Distinguish **못** and **안** from the context
* Use both forms in simple Korean sentences

## Tiếng Việt So Sánh (Vietnamese Comparison)

Vietnamese commonly uses **không** for different kinds of negation:

* Tôi không đi học. — I don't go to school.
* Tôi không thể đi học. — I cannot go to school.

Korean distinguishes these meanings more clearly:

* **나는 학교에 안 가요.**
  — I don't go to school. / I choose not to go.

* **나는 학교에 못 가요.**
  — I cannot go to school. / Something prevents me from going.

The context tells us whether the person **chooses not to do something** or **cannot do it**.

## Khái Niệm (The Concept)

### 안 — Not doing something

**안** is used when the subject does not do an action.

The reason may be a choice, preference, habit, or simple negative statement.

```text
안 + Verb

안 가요 = don't go
안 먹어요 = don't eat
안 공부해요 = don't study
```

Example:

```text
나는 오늘 학교에 안 가요.
I am not going to school today.
```

This sentence does not say that the speaker is unable to go. The speaker simply says that they are not going.

### 못 — Cannot do something

**못** is used when the subject cannot perform an action because of ability, circumstances, conditions, or opportunity.

```text
못 + Verb

못 가요 = cannot go
못 먹어요 = cannot eat
못 공부해요 = cannot study
```

Example:

```text
나는 아파서 학교에 못 가요.
I cannot go to school because I am sick.
```

The speaker wants or needs to go, but a circumstance prevents it.

## Cấu Trúc (Structure)

### Basic pattern

```text
Subject + 안 + Verb

나는 안 가요.
I don't go.
```

```text
Subject + 못 + Verb

나는 못 가요.
I cannot go.
```

Both **안** and **못** come before the verb.

## Quy Tắc (Rules)

### Rule 1: 안 comes before the verb

```text
나는 밥을 안 먹어요.
I don't eat rice.
```

### Rule 2: 못 comes before the verb

```text
나는 밥을 못 먹어요.
I cannot eat rice.
```

### Rule 3: The context determines the meaning

Compare:

```text
나는 오늘 학교에 안 가요.
I am not going to school today.
```

Possible reason:

* I choose not to go.
* I don't plan to go.
* I simply state that I am not going.

Now compare:

```text
나는 오늘 학교에 못 가요.
I cannot go to school today.
```

Possible reason:

* I am sick.
* I have no way to get there.
* Another circumstance prevents me from going.

## 예제 (Examples)

### Example 1: Going to school

```text
나는 학교에 안 가요.
I don't go to school.
```

The sentence expresses **not going**.

```text
나는 학교에 못 가요.
I cannot go to school.
```

The sentence expresses **inability or circumstances preventing the action**.

### Example 2: Speaking Korean

```text
나는 한국어를 안 말해요.
I don't speak Korean.
```

The speaker is simply not speaking Korean.

```text
나는 한국어를 못 말해요.
I cannot speak Korean.
```

The speaker cannot speak Korean, for example because they have not learned enough Korean.

### Example 3: Eating

```text
나는 고기를 안 먹어요.
I don't eat meat.
```

This can describe a choice, preference, or habit.

```text
나는 고기를 못 먹어요.
I cannot eat meat.
```

This can mean that the person cannot eat it because of a condition or circumstance.

## 못 vs 안 비교

|                      | 안                          | 못                        |
| -------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Basic meaning        | not / don't                | cannot                   |
| Main idea            | The action does not happen | The action cannot happen |
| Choice               | Often possible             | Not the main meaning     |
| Ability/circumstance | Not emphasized             | Important                |
| Example              | 학교에 안 가요                   | 학교에 못 가요                 |

### 기억하기 (Remember)

```text
안 → 안 한다
I don't do it.

못 → 할 수 없다
I cannot do it.
```

A useful question is:

**“Does the person simply not do it, or can the person not do it?”**

* Simply not doing → **안**
* Unable to do → **못**

## 흔한 실수 (Common Errors)

### Mistake 1: Using 못 when you mean a choice

❌ 나는 공부를 못 해요.
If you mean “I don't study because I don't want to,” this is not the best choice.

✓ 나는 공부를 안 해요.
I don't study.

### Mistake 2: Using 안 when something prevents the action

If you mean that you cannot study because you are sick:

✓ 나는 아파서 공부를 못 해요.
I cannot study because I am sick.

### Mistake 3: Putting the negation after the verb

❌ 나는 가요 못.

✓ 나는 못 가요.

The negation marker comes before the verb.

## 연습 (Practice)

Choose **안** or **못** according to the meaning.

### Practice 1

The student does not want to eat breakfast.

```text
나는 아침을 ___ 먹어요.
```

Answer: **안**

### Practice 2

The student is sick and cannot go to school.

```text
나는 아파서 학교에 ___ 가요.
```

Answer: **못**

### Practice 3

A student has not learned Korean and cannot speak it.

```text
저는 한국어를 ___ 말해요.
```

Answer: **못**

### Practice 4

A student chooses not to study today.

```text
오늘은 공부를 ___ 해요.
```

Answer: **안**

## 요약 (Summary)

The key distinction is:

* **안** = not doing something
* **못** = cannot do something

Both normally appear before the verb:

```text
안 + Verb
못 + Verb
```

Compare:

```text
나는 학교에 안 가요.
I don't go to school.

나는 학교에 못 가요.
I cannot go to school.
```

The most important skill is to understand the **context**.
