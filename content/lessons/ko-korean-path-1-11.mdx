# 미래 계획과 의도: -(으)ㄹ 거예요

## 목표 (Mục Tiêu / Learning Objectives)

After this lesson, you will:

* Understand **-(으)ㄹ 거예요** as a pattern for future plans and intentions
* Talk about what you plan or intend to do
* Make simple statements about future actions
* Distinguish future plans from present actions
* Use **-(으)ㄹ 거예요** in simple Korean sentences

## Tiếng Việt So Sánh (Vietnamese Comparison)

Vietnamese commonly uses expressions such as **“sẽ”, “định”, “dự định”** when talking about future actions.

For example:

* Tôi sẽ đi học ngày mai. — I will go to school tomorrow.
* Tôi định học tiếng Hàn. — I plan to study Korean.
* Tôi sẽ gặp bạn vào cuối tuần. — I will meet my friend on the weekend.

Korean commonly uses **-(으)ㄹ 거예요** to express future plans, intentions, or predictions.

* **내일 학교에 갈 거예요.**
  — I will go to school tomorrow.

* **한국어를 공부할 거예요.**
  — I will study Korean.

* **주말에 친구를 만날 거예요.**
  — I will meet my friend on the weekend.

The context helps determine whether the sentence means a **plan/intention** or a **prediction**.

## Khái Niệm (The Concept)

### -(으)ㄹ 거예요 — Future plans and intentions

**-(으)ㄹ 거예요** is commonly used when the speaker talks about something that will happen in the future.

It is especially useful when talking about a plan or intention.

```text
Verb + -(으)ㄹ 거예요

갈 거예요 = will go
먹을 거예요 = will eat
공부할 거예요 = will study
```

Example:

```text
내일 공부할 거예요.
I will study tomorrow.
```

The speaker is talking about a future action or plan.

### Talking about someone else's future action

The same pattern can also describe another person's expected future action.

```text
민수는 내일 학교에 갈 거예요.
Minsu will go to school tomorrow.
```

The speaker expects or knows that Minsu will go to school.

## Cấu Trúc (Structure)

### Basic pattern

```text
Verb stem + -(으)ㄹ 거예요
```

If the verb stem has **no final consonant (받침)**:

```text
가다 → 갈 거예요
보다 → 볼 거예요
공부하다 → 공부할 거예요
```

If the verb stem has a **final consonant (받침)**:

```text
먹다 → 먹을 거예요
읽다 → 읽을 거예요
앉다 → 앉을 거예요
```

### Verbs ending in ㄹ

If the verb stem ends in **ㄹ**, use **-ㄹ 거예요**.

```text
살다 → 살 거예요
만들다 → 만들 거예요
열다 → 열 거예요
```

Do not add **-을 거예요** after ㄹ.

## Quy Tắc (Rules)

### Rule 1: No final consonant → -ㄹ 거예요

```text
가다 → 갈 거예요.
I will go.
```

```text
보다 → 볼 거예요.
I will watch.
```

### Rule 2: Final consonant → -을 거예요

```text
먹다 → 먹을 거예요.
I will eat.
```

```text
읽다 → 읽을 거예요.
I will read.
```

### Rule 3: Final consonant ㄹ → -ㄹ 거예요

```text
만들다 → 만들 거예요.
I will make it.
```

```text
열다 → 열 거예요.
I will open it.
```

## 예제 (Examples)

### Example 1: Future plans

```text
내일 학교에 갈 거예요.
I will go to school tomorrow.
```

The speaker is talking about a future plan.

```text
주말에 친구를 만날 거예요.
I will meet my friend on the weekend.
```

The meeting is planned for the future.

### Example 2: Studying Korean

```text
저는 한국어를 공부할 거예요.
I will study Korean.
```

The speaker has a plan or intention to study Korean.

```text
오늘 저녁에 숙제를 할 거예요.
I will do my homework this evening.
```

The homework is planned for later.

### Example 3: Future prediction

```text
내일 비가 올 거예요.
It will rain tomorrow.
```

Here, **-(으)ㄹ 거예요** expresses a prediction rather than the speaker's personal plan.

```text
아마 늦을 거예요.
It will probably be late.
```

The speaker predicts a future situation.

### 기억하기 (Remember)

```text
-(으)ㄹ 거예요 → future
```

A useful question is:

**“Is this talking about something that will happen in the future?”**

* Future plan → **-(으)ㄹ 거예요**
* Future prediction → **-(으)ㄹ 거예요**

The context tells us whether it is a plan or prediction.

## 흔한 실수 (Common Errors)

### Mistake 1: Using the present tense for a clear future plan

If you want to clearly express a future plan:

❌ 내일 학교에 가요.

This can sometimes mean “I am going to school tomorrow,” but it does not specifically teach the future pattern.

✓ 내일 학교에 갈 거예요.

I will go to school tomorrow.

### Mistake 2: Using -을 after a vowel

❌ 가을 거예요.

✓ 갈 거예요.

The verb **가다** has no final consonant, so use **-ㄹ 거예요**.

### Mistake 3: Adding 을 after ㄹ

❌ 만들을 거예요.

✓ 만들 거예요.

The verb stem **만들-** already ends in ㄹ.

### Mistake 4: Forgetting the space before 거예요

The correct form is written with a space:

✓ **갈 거예요.**

Not:

❌ **갈거예요.**

**거예요** is written separately from the preceding verb form.

## 요약 (Summary)

The key idea is:

* **-(으)ㄹ 거예요** = will / be going to
* It is commonly used for future plans and intentions.
* It can also express a prediction about the future.
* The verb stem determines whether to use **-ㄹ** or **-을**.

Basic patterns:

```text
No final consonant → -ㄹ 거예요

가다 → 갈 거예요
보다 → 볼 거예요
```

```text
Final consonant → -을 거예요

먹다 → 먹을 거예요
읽다 → 읽을 거예요
```

```text
Final consonant ㄹ → -ㄹ 거예요

만들다 → 만들 거예요
열다 → 열 거예요
```

Compare:

```text
저는 한국어를 공부해요.
I study Korean.

저는 한국어를 공부할 거예요.
I will study Korean.
```

The most important skill is to recognize that **-(으)ㄹ 거예요** connects an action or situation to the **future**, especially a plan, intention, or prediction.
